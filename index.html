01 / About

A senior delivery leader who switches gears.

—

I lead complex, cross functional delivery from a people first perspective, building frameworks that enable great work rather than constrain it. As an agile transformation leader with 25 years across gaming, tech, and enterprise consulting, I bridge the gap between technical teams, commercial partners, and CIO level leaders to keep ambitious projects moving.

I have owned delivery pipelines end to end across console, PC, live entertainment, and risk management inside regulated banks. I balance high level governance with practical, hands on execution in Jira and modern workflows to ensure teams ship reliably.

I adapt my approach to suit the audience, serving as an effective senior interface who protects project scope, provides partner clarity, and drives rapid decision making. I am also an active AI user, helping organisations scale delivery efficiency with the understanding that what holds enterprise AI back is governance, funding, and decision rights, not the tooling itself.
